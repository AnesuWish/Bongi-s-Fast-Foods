Our Story

Founded in 2018 from a humble kitchen in Boksburg with a simple dream, Bongi's Fast Foods was born out of a passion for serving mouthwatering, flame-grilled classics without the long wait. What started as a single local hotspot has since grown into a beloved neighborhood fixture, driven by our unwavering commitment to quality ingredients, pristine kitchens, and exceptionally friendly service. We strictly partner with local farms to ensure our beef is always fresh and our produce is crisp, delivering a premium dining experience at an everyday value. As we continue to expand, our core focus remains unchanged: treating our team like family, investing back into the local communities that support us, and serving up happiness, one perfect meal at a time.





Meet Our Team

At Bongi’s Fast Food, our team is the secret ingredient behind every sizzle, crunch, and smile. Led by our visionary founder, Bongi Ndlovu, our passionate crew brings authentic local flavor and energy to the kitchen every single day. Head chef Sipho Khumalo keeps the grills blazing with his secret spice blends, while Thabo Mokoena ensures our famous hand-cut chips are fried to golden perfection. Out front, Lerato Patel and Chantel de Klerk greet every customer with the warmest South African hospitality and lightning-fast service. We are more than just a kitchen crew; we are a family dedicated to serving up pure happiness in a box.



What Bongi's Stands For

At Bongi’s Fast Food, we do not just flip burgers; we serve our community with pride. Our core values guide every meal we prep: Freshness First means using crisp, locally sourced ingredients daily; Speedy Service ensures your food arrives piping hot without the wait; and Affordable Flavours guarantees everyone gets a delicious, hearty meal at a fair price. We believe in keeping our kitchens spotless, our smiles bright, and our recipes undeniably local.
