Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes! Bongi's delivers straight to your door through our delivery partners. Check our app to see if you are in our delivery zone.


Are there vegetarian or vegan options on the menu?

Absolutely. We serve a delicious Bongi's Veggie Burger and our regular chips are 100% vegetarian-friendly.


How can I apply to work at Bongi's?

You can check out our Careers page to view open positions, or send your CV directly to jobs@bongis.com.


Do you cater for large parties or events?

Yes, we do! Please fill out our main enquiry form with your event details, guest count, and date, and we will get back to you with platter pricing.


What are your payment methods?

We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, mobile payments, and digital wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay.


Do you have allergen information available?

Yes, we maintain detailed allergen information for all menu items. Please ask our staff or check our website for a complete allergen guide.


What is your return or refund policy?

If you are unsatisfied with your meal, we will remake it or offer a full refund. Your satisfaction is guaranteed!


Do you offer loyalty programs or rewards?

Absolutely! Download our BurgerCraze mobile app to earn reward points on every purchase and access exclusive deals.


What are your operating hours?

Monday-Thursday: 10:00 AM - 10:00 PM | Friday-Saturday: 8:00 AM - 12:00 AM | Sunday: 12:00 PM - 10:00 PM
