Project Overview

Bongi's Fast Food is a local fast-food restaurant that serves freshly made meals, including burgers, chicken, chips, pizzas, wraps, ice cream and beverages. The business started in the owner's kitchen in Bonginkosi in 2018 and eventually moved to its own shop in Boksburg, Johannesburg, in 2022. The proposed website will increase the restaurant's online visibility and make it easier for customers to view the menu, find current promotions and specials, and access contact and store location information.

The target audience includes people in the East Rand region, students at nearby tertiary institutions, and people who need catering services for events. Vision To become the best fast-food restaurant in town by delivering exceptional service and delicious, affordable and high-quality meals to customers.

Mission To prepare fresh, affordable and tasty meals while providing friendly service in a clean and lively environment.

Website Goals and Objectives The website aims to: Increase Bongi's Fast Food's online visibility. Allow potential customers to view the menu online. Display current promotions and specials. Provide contact details and the restaurant's location. Promote catering and other services. Increase website visitors. Increase the number of customer enquiries. Achieve customer ratings of at least 80%.

Key Features and Functionality Home Page The homepage will introduce customers to Bongi's Fast Food and provide information about the restaurant's main offers. About Us The About Us page will include: A brief history of the restaurant. The business vision. The business mission. General information about the restaurant. Services

The Services page will explain the services offered by the business, including: Catering services. Party venue. Fast food. Beverages. Contact Us

The Contact Us page will provide: Contact information. The physical location of the restaurant. A map to the restaurant. Menu

The website will allow customers to view the restaurant's food and beverage options online. Promotions and Specials The website will display current promotions and specials to attract customers and encourage visits. Navigation The website will have a simple navigation menu with links to: Home Services About Us Contact Us Enquiries The navigation will be simple and easy to use. Design and User Experience The website will have a clean, professional, lively and vibrant appearance. Colours The main website colours will be: Red Yellow White Grey These colours will create a clean, exciting and vibrant fast-food look and feel.

Typography Poppins Bold - headings. Poppins Semi-Bold - subheadings. Open Sans Regular - body text.

The website will use simple navigation, readable content and fast page loading to make the user's experience efficient. Technical Requirements
